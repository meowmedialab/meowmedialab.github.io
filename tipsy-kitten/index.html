Tipsy Kitten

A cocktail companion for Android.

Tipsy Kitten helps you keep track of the bottles, mixers, and garnishes you own, then surfaces drinks you can actually make tonight. Save your own recipes, rate the ones you've tried, and (optionally) sign in with Google to sync across devices.
